Upload Event Photos

Users have access to the Upload Folder option. Where they enter the event or folder name and the event date. After that, they can upload an entire folder containing multiple photos from the event.

All photos are stored in a centralized system under the selected event, making them easy to manage and search later. This feature works the same way for everyone, ensuring a simple and consistent upload experience.

Search Photos by Face

Users can search for photos by entering the event or folder name. Matching events are displayed, and the user can open the desired folder.

Once inside, the camera opens so the user can scan their face or upload a selfie. The system then runs face recognition by comparing the user’s face with all photos in that event.

Only the photos that match the user’s face are shown, ensuring privacy and security. Users can download only their own matched photos, keeping personal data protected.

Face Snapper lets you easily upload event photos, search them with face recognition, and instantly find your memories — fast, secure, and personalized.
